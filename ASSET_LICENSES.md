# Asset and Third-Party License Record

This repository may contain project-specific and third-party visual assets.

Rover geometry, meshes, and related files may originate from project or laboratory work.

Redistribution permission should be confirmed before public distribution.

## BlenderKit and Other External Assets

Some materials and textures used during the Blender workflow may originate from external asset libraries.

The applicable license for every redistributed asset must be verified before making the repository public.

## Terrain Assets

The terrain and environmental assets are used as Mars-inspired simulation assets.

The original source and redistribution rights of externally sourced assets should be verified.

## NASA / Planetary Data

Where NASA or other planetary datasets are incorporated, the relevant source and applicable terms should be acknowledged.

## Important Rule

Do not assume that an asset being available for use in a modelling application automatically grants permission to redistribute the original mesh or texture files in a public repository.

Assets with unclear redistribution rights should remain outside the public repository.
