# Research Notes

## Research Position

The rover is treated as a mobile sensing element rather than solely as a mobility platform.

A representative application context is infrastructure inspection or surface monitoring.

## Experimental Variables

Potential controlled variables include:

- gravity
- surface friction
- rover mass
- terrain configuration
- obstacle density
- sensing configuration

## Candidate Measurements

- displacement
- travel time
- linear velocity
- wheel angular velocity
- pitch
- roll
- IMU acceleration
- IMU angular velocity
- LiDAR output rate
- point-cloud observations
- camera output rate

## Scientific Boundaries

The environment is Mars-inspired rather than an exact reconstruction of Jezero Crater.

Visual materials are intended to provide plausible environmental appearance and perception structure, not exact mineralogical or radiometric measurements.

The simulated RoboSense and ZED configurations are hardware-inspired simulation models rather than hardware-calibrated digital twins.

## Future Directions

- controlled experimental comparison
- sensor fusion
- autonomous navigation
- SLAM
- terrain classification
- domain randomization
- sim-to-real analysis
- long-duration surface-operation studies
